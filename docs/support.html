Common questions

Why does the app ask for location?
Sun & Moon uses location to show local weather, sunrise, sunset, moon, and time zone data.

Can I use the app without location access?
Yes. The app should remain usable with a fallback location if you do not grant access.

Where is my data stored?
Birth data and saved location data are stored locally on your device.

What if weather data is unavailable?
Weather data depends on Apple WeatherKit and network availability.
