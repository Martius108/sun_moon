# Privacy Policy for Sun & Moon

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Sun & Moon is an iOS app by Martin Lanius. This privacy policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## Information Used by the App

Sun & Moon may use your current location to show local weather, sunrise, sunset, moon, and time zone information.

The app may also store birth date, birth time, city, latitude, and longitude information locally on your device. This information is used to calculate zodiac and ascendant information inside the app.

## Weather Data

Weather information is provided through Apple WeatherKit. When the app requests weather data, location information may be sent to Apple so WeatherKit can return weather, sun, and moon related data for that location.

## Geocoding

When you use the "Get Coordinates" feature, the city name you enter may be sent to Apple's geocoding service so the app can determine latitude and longitude for that city.

## Data Storage

Birth data and saved location information are stored locally on your device with SwiftData. Sun & Moon does not synchronize this data through CloudKit and does not operate its own server for it.

## Tracking and Advertising

Sun & Moon does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Sun & Moon does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Sun & Moon does not sell your personal data.

## Data Sharing

Sun & Moon does not share your locally saved birth data with data brokers or advertising networks.

Weather requests are handled through Apple WeatherKit as described above.

City lookup requests are handled through Apple's geocoding service as described above.
